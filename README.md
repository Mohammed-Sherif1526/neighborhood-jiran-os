# 🏘️ Neighborhood OS

> A Smart Neighborhood Management Platform that connects residents, building managers, and local service providers in one organized digital ecosystem.

---

## 📖 Overview

Neighborhood OS is a UX/UI graduation project that aims to improve communication and service management inside residential communities.

Instead of relying on scattered WhatsApp groups or social media, Neighborhood OS provides a centralized platform where residents can report issues, track requests, receive announcements, participate in polls, and engage with their neighborhood.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Managing neighborhood services is often inefficient due to:

- Scattered communication channels.
- Lack of transparency.
- No centralized issue tracking.
- Limited resident participation.
- Slow response to community problems.

These challenges reduce engagement and make neighborhood management difficult.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Neighborhood OS transforms neighborhood management into a digital experience by providing one organized platform where users can:

- Report issues
- Track requests
- Receive announcements
- Participate in community polls
- Share neighborhood resources
- Request help during emergencies

---

## 👥 Target Users

### 🏠 Residents
Residents can report issues, follow request status, receive updates, and participate in community activities.

### 🏢 Building Managers
Managers can monitor requests, assign tasks, and communicate efficiently with residents.

### 🛠️ Service Providers
Service providers receive assigned requests and update their progress until completion.
